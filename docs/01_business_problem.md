# Business Problem

## Background

The company has a large amount of customer transaction data, but raw transaction records do not clearly show how customers are behaving over time.

## Business Problem

The company needs to understand its customer base and identify different types of customers based on their purchasing behavior.

In particular, the business wants to identify:

* High-value customers who purchase frequently and recently
* Loyal and active customers
* Customers whose purchasing activity has decreased
* Customers who have become inactive
* Customers who may need targeted re-engagement

Without customer segmentation, it is difficult for the business to create appropriate strategies for different customer groups.

## Objective

Use customer transaction data and RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) analysis to segment customers based on their purchasing behavior and generate actionable business insights.

## Key Business Questions

1. Who are the most valuable customers?
2. Which customers are highly engaged and loyal?
3. Which customers have not purchased recently?
4. Which customer groups may require re-engagement?
5. Which customer segments contribute the most revenue?
6. What marketing strategies could be considered for each customer segment?
